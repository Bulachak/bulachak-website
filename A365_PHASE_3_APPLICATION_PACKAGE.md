## 1. TASK 1: Business Idea Narrative

**Portal Requirement:** Business idea description.  
**Word Count:** 183 words (Limit: 200 words).  
**Source:** Authoritative September 7, 2026 draft (*The best A4A problem description yet - by Gemini Spark*).

Across sport, human devotion is quietly drained by operational friction. From national governing bodies and grassroots academies to retiring athletes launching new ventures, people give their lives to athletic excellence, only to spend their days fighting disconnected spreadsheets, fragmented compliance, and administrative overload. Passion gets buried under operational debt.

A4A (Agents for Athletes) is the commercial expression of Bulachak, built on the principle that true value is grown, not extracted. We deliver an integrated, agent-run back office as Service-as-Software. Instead of forcing sports leaders to become software operators, coordinated AI agents execute intake, seasonal scheduling, billing reconciliation, and governance workflows behind one plain-language interface. We run the operational spine so coaches can mentor, athletes can train, and directors can lead with focus.

Because sport only flourishes through shared reciprocity, A4A embeds its purpose directly in its financial structure: an unconditional four percent gross revenue pledge to Parafencing 4 Progress (P4P), funding adaptive equipment access and competitive pipelines ahead of LA28. The impact: resilient sports organizations freed from administrative burnout, sustainably growing sovereign equity for adaptive athletes.

## 2. TASK 2: Business Model Canvas (Six Official Questions)

### 2.1 Value Proposition
**Word Count:** 96 words (Limit: 100 words).

A4A gives small sport organizations and athlete-led businesses the practical digital operations they cannot afford in-house. We deliver done-for-you operational sprints that modernize websites, automate inquiry intake, install practical AI workflows for recurring administration, and package compelling sponsor materials. Clients do not buy software they must struggle to learn. They buy completed outcomes: hours returned to coaching, streamlined athlete registration, and partner-ready professional infrastructure. A4A is differentiated by founder-market fit as an Olympic medalist, proven delivery across civic and youth sports, and our built-in four percent revenue pledge to Parafencing 4 Progress.

### 2.2 Target Customers
**Word Count:** 139 words (Limit: 150 words).

A4A serves three concentric circles across the sports ecosystem that share one structural problem: small teams doing everything by hand with no dedicated operations staff.

1. Athlete-founders: Transitioning and dual-career elite athletes building coaching academies, sports camps, consulting practices, or sporting goods brands.

2. Grassroots clubs and community programs: Local sports organizations run by passionate coaches who are forced to double as full-time administrators for scheduling, billing, parent inquiries, and compliance.

3. Para-sport and adaptive sport organizations: Adaptive programs that face intense operational demands, specialized equipment sourcing barriers, and detailed grant and sponsor reporting requirements.

Our starting line focuses on athlete-led ventures and adaptive sport programs, where the administrative burden is heaviest and community impact is most acute. Federations and governing bodies represent an institutional channel that can sponsor operational sprint cohorts for their transitioning athletes.

### 2.3 Main Activities
**Word Count:** 135 words (Limit: 150 words).

My core activity is delivering focused, done-for-you operational sprints and structured cohort programs rather than selling software licenses:

Operational diagnostics: Auditing a sports organization's inquiry pathways, website responsiveness, scheduling friction, and recurring administrative bottlenecks.

System implementation: Building mobile-first web pages, automating registration and digital liability waivers, configuring payment routing, and deploying practical AI workflow templates for parent communications, scheduling follow-ups, and sponsor proposals.

Seasonal event operations: Setting up high-converting registration funnels, automated reminder sequences, and roster management for sports camps and tournaments.

Cohort delivery: Facilitating 6-week operational accelerators for federation-sponsored cohorts, providing group workshops and 1-on-1 implementation sprints.

Adaptive equipment sourcing: Connecting adaptive sport programs with compliant wholesale manufacturers via Alibaba.com.

Case study synthesis: Documenting measurable outcomes, including hours saved and enrollment growth, validating repeatable models across the sports ecosystem.

### 2.4 Revenue Streams
**Word Count:** 142 words (Limit: 150 words).

A4A generates revenue through three productized service streams aligned with sports budgeting cycles, avoiding hourly consulting and monthly retainers:

1. Turnkey Sport Ops Sprint: Fixed-scope 21-day digital modernizations for clubs and athlete-founders, delivering websites, intake automation, and custom AI communication templates with complete ownership ($2,800 to $3,000).

2. Seasonal Camp and Event Launches: Performance-aligned operational launches for summer camps, clinics, and tournaments with a $1,000 base fee plus value-share per registered athlete.

3. Sponsored Cohort Accelerators: Six-week structured digital modernizations sponsored by sports federations, city recreation departments, and corporate partners for cohorts of 6 to 8 athlete-founders or adaptive clubs ($15,000 to $18,000).

Sequencing prioritizes individual club sprints to build immediate cash flow before expanding institutional contracts. Four percent of all gross revenue is pledged unconditionally to Parafencing for Progress, directly funding wheelchair fencing access and fair coach pay.


### 2.5 Success Factors
**Word Count:** 140 words (Limit: 150 words).

Founder-market credibility: I am an Olympic silver medalist in fencing, interim US Parafencing National Team Coach, and former Chair of USA Fencing's Hall of Fame Committee. I have lived the administrative struggle at every level of sport.

Proven delivery record: Real systems already delivered, including the PlayLA Flag Football bilingual web app for the City of Los Angeles across 15 active sites; the Engineer History AI Concierge with 70% inquiry deflection; an operations stack for an 80-athlete youth sports academy; and USA Fencing governance systems.

Practical delivery model: We do the heavy lifting. Delivering working systems instead of complicated software removes the primary adoption barrier for busy coaches and athletes.

Strategic partner alignment: Using Alibaba.com's B2B sourcing infrastructure to solve equipment cost barriers in adaptive sport directly aligns with the Accelerator's founding partner.

Direct social mission: Connecting commercial revenue directly to parafencing creates authentic purpose and partner trust.

### 2.6 Key Risks
**Word Count:** 141 words (Limit: 150 words).

Stage risk: A4A is an early-stage venture validating its productized pricing. Mitigation: Starting with small, fixed-price paid pilots before making large enterprise commitments.

Solo-founder capacity: Delivering hands-on sprints as a solo founder limits initial scale. Mitigation: Packaging standardized workflow templates and certifying trained transitioning athletes as delivery partners.

Skepticism of technology: Sports directors are fatigued by complicated apps and artificial intelligence hype. Mitigation: Focusing strictly on practical time savings and tangible deliverables rather than tech jargon.

Institutional sales latency: Sports federations and governing bodies move slowly. Mitigation: Keeping athlete and grassroots club sprints financially viable and independent of institutional sales cycles.

Data privacy and compliance: Managing youth sports registrations and parent communications requires strict privacy standards. Mitigation: Enforcing conservative data protection protocols and keeping sensitive athlete and payment data out of public AI models.
