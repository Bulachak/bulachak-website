## 5. Verbatim Sales Objection Handling Scripts

Drawn from `value-based-pricing-sales` (Handling the Pricing Question Without Losing the Deal) and `ai-battlecard-builder`.

### Objection 1: "How much does the sprint cost?" (Early in conversation)
> **Script:**  
> "I want to give you an exact figure, but the scope depends on your specific setup. Some clubs only need an inquiry triage overhaul, while others need registration checkout and sponsor packaging. May I ask two quick questions about your current administrative bottlenecks first? That way, if we are not a fit, I will tell you immediately; and if we are, I can give you the exact price that matches your needs."

### Objection 2: "We do not have the budget for consulting right now."
> **Script:**  
> "I completely respect that, especially in grassroots sports where every dollar matters. May I ask: roughly how many hours a week do you or your coaches spend answering repetitive emails, chasing late registrations, and updating spreadsheets? Most directors tell us it is 12 to 15 hours. At a modest $25 an hour, that is over $1,200 every month lost to administrative drag, not to mention families who give up when inquiries go unanswered for two days.  
> 
> Our 14-day sprint costs $1,800 once. It eliminates those bottlenecks and installs systems that run permanently. Would you invest $1,800 once to eliminate an ongoing $14,000 yearly operational drain?"

### Objection 3: "We already use software like TeamSnap or Mindbody."
> **Script:**  
> "Those are solid tools for basic rosters. We do not replace your core management software; we make it actually work for you. Most clubs use only 10% of their software features because the apps do not triage incoming parent inquiries, write sponsor proposals, or follow up with at-risk athletes who miss three practices in a row.  
> 
> We build the operational glue around your existing tools using practical AI workflows, so your software stops being a chore and starts operating as a true assistant."

### Objection 4: "We are not tech-savvy and AI sounds intimidating or risky for youth data."
> **Script:**  
> "You do not need to be tech-savvy at all; that is exactly why A4A exists. You will never touch code or complex prompts. We build simple workflows that work through your existing email or messaging tools.  
> 
> Furthermore, we operate under strict privacy-first protocols: we never feed youth, medical, or payment data into public AI models. Everything is structured under private, verified templates that protect your athletes and keep you 100% compliant."

- **Day 3:** Operational Bottleneck Audit delivered (pinpointing top 3 friction areas).
- **Day 5:** Website and inquiry path optimization (mobile responsive check, clear registration CTAs).
- **Day 7:** Milestone Review 1 (approving digital assets and workflow specifications).

### Week 2 (Days 8 to 14): AI Workflow Deployment & Partner Readiness
- **Day 8:** Deploy Workflow 1 (Automated Lead & Inquiry Triage).
- **Day 10:** Deploy Workflow 2 (Member Onboarding & Retention Check-in Sequence).
- **Day 12:** Deploy Workflow 3 (Sponsorship Brief & Grant Storytelling Engine).
- **Day 14:** Sprint Completion & Walkthrough (Handoff of 1-Page Sport Partner Battlecard and staff training).

### Weeks 3 and 4 (Days 15 to 30): Monitored Execution & Impact Verification
- **Day 21:** 7-day post-launch check-in (fine-tuning templates based on real incoming inquiries).
- **Day 30:** Final Impact Assessment (measuring administrative hours saved, new inquiries captured, and documenting adaptive sport access metrics).
