## Executive Summary: The Core Question

During the September 16, 2026 strategic check-in, Anvar Tuykin posed the two foundational Customer Development questions before building any strategy:

> 1. **«Кому и зачем это нужно?»** (Who needs this and why?)  
> 2. **«Где там деньги / Откуда деньги / На какие деньги это будет существовать?»** (Where is the money / how does it fund itself?)

This document records the grounded, mathematically verified internal answers across the three branches of Bulachak, mapping them directly against Anvar's 4-hypothesis framework:
* **Гипотеза проблемы** (Problem Hypothesis)
* **Гипотеза решения** (Solution Hypothesis — Service-as-Software, NOT SaaS)
* **Гипотеза ценности** (Value & Unit Economics Hypothesis)
* **Гипотеза роста** (Growth & Go-To-Market Hypothesis)

## 1. Branch 1: A4A (Agents 4 Athletes) — The Commercial Engine

### A. Кому это нужно? (Target Audience)
1. **Grassroots Sports Clubs & Academies (20–100 athletes):** Fencing salles, martial arts dojos, gymnastics schools, youth tennis clubs in the US that run on private tuition and parent fees without municipal subsidies.
2. **Transitioning & Dual-Career Athlete-Founders:** Elite/Olympic competitors launching private academies, clinics, or sports consulting practices, possessing deep sport authority but zero administrative infrastructure.
3. **Institutional Sports Bodies (B2G/B2B):** Municipal recreation departments (e.g., City of Los Angeles / PlayLA) and National Governing Bodies (e.g., USA Fencing) needing to modernize member clubs or transition athlete cohorts.

### B. Зачем это нужно? (Problem vs. Solution)
* **The Burning Problem:** Head coaches and founders spend **10 to 15+ hours per week** on manual operational drag: untangling parent messages across WhatsApp/SMS/Instagram, chasing monthly tuition, collecting paper liability waivers, and fixing spreadsheet errors.
* **Why Traditional Solutions Fail:**
  * *SaaS Platforms:* High cognitive overhead. Busy coaches suffer from software fatigue; they buy subscriptions, get overwhelmed by complex settings, and abandon them.
  * *Hiring Staff:* A full-time or part-time office manager in the US costs $30,000–$60,000/year plus taxes, which small clubs cannot afford.
* **The Solution (Service-as-Software & AI-Native Back Office):**
  * A4A does **not** sell software licenses or logins.
  * A4A delivers a **completed operational spine** via a 14–21 day Turnkey Sport Ops Sprint:
    1. *Digital Front Door:* Fast, mobile-responsive registration page.
    2. *Automated Intake:* Digital liability waivers and integrated payment routing (Stripe/ACH).
    3. *AI-Native Concierge:* Specialized agent trained on club rules, schedule, and pricing, deflecting up to 70% of repetitive parent inquiries.
    4. *Clean Hand-off:* 100% asset ownership transferred to the client with zero mandatory recurring vendor fees.

### C. Где деньги? (Revenue Model & Unit Economics)
* **Productized Revenue Pillars:**
  1. **Turnkey Sport Ops Sprint:** Fixed flat fee of **$2,800 to $3,000** (50% deposit upfront, 50% upon milestone hand-off).
  2. **Seasonal Camp & Event Value-Share:** **$1,000 base fee + 6% to 8% of gross camp tuition** ($15–$25 per registered athlete), aligning payment with seasonal cash inflow.
  3. **Sponsored Cohort Accelerators:** **$15,000 to $18,000** per 6-week cohort (6–8 participating organizations), funded by federation grants or corporate partners.
* **Founder Personal Economics (Los Angeles Baseline):**
  * Target delivery: **2 Turnkey Sprints per month** = $6,000 gross revenue.
  * Less 4% P4P Social Pledge: -$240.00
  * Less Operating Overhead (AI compute, cloud, tools): -$450.00
  * Less Payment Processing (Stripe/ACH blend ~2.9%): -$174.00
  * **Net Monthly Cash Flow to Founder:** **~$5,136.00 / month**.
  * **Workload:** Capped at **14 to 18 hours per week** (7 to 9 hours per sprint), safeguarding dedicated time for Team USA national coaching and training.

## 2. Branch 2: P4P (Parafencing 4 Progress) — The Adaptive Sport Mission

### A. Кому это нужно? (Target Audience)
* Adaptive athletes with mobility impairments seeking competitive, accessible fencing training in the United States.
* The US Paralympic Movement preparing for the home Games at **LA28**.

### B. Зачем это нужно? (Problem vs. Solution)
* **The Vacuum:** Parafencing in the US historically suffered from an administrative and coaching vacuum. The interim National Coach post was vacant because compensation was minimal and logistics were daunting.
* **Equipment Barrier:** Specialized fencing wheelchairs cost $5,000+ each, plus custom anchoring frames and personal-care assistant (PCA) travel expenses.
* **The Solution:** A dedicated nonprofit vehicle building sovereign equipment pipelines, coach development frameworks, and training camps ahead of LA28.

### C. Где деньги? (Funding Architecture)
1. **Unconditional 4% Commercial Revenue Pledge:** 4% of every dollar earned by A4A flows directly into P4P, creating an automatic, non-charity-dependent baseline.
2. **Global Sourcing Arbitrage (Alibaba.com):** Direct factory relationships via the IOC Athlete365 Business Accelerator to source compliant wheelchairs and frames at wholesale cost, reducing capital expenses by 50–60%.
3. **Institutional Grants & Corporate CSR:** LA28 community sports legacy funds, USOPC adaptive sport grants, and corporate sponsorships.

## 3. Branch 3: I4I (Immigrant 4 Immigrant) — Radical Reciprocity Network

### A. Кому это нужно? (Target Audience)
* Immigrant founders, independent service professionals, and small business operators navigating high assimilation hurdles and fragmented diaspora connections in the US.
* Small sports academies seeking collaborative cross-promotions (e.g., local physiotherapists, nutritionists, language tutors).

### B. Зачем это нужно? (Problem vs. Solution)
* **The Problem:** Small businesses cannot afford enterprise agency retainers or sponsorships with major leagues. Simultaneously, immigrant communities often suffer from insular silos or zero-sum competition rather than collaborative leverage.
* **The Inversion:** Inverting «an eye for an eye» into **Radical Reciprocity** — mutual uplift, peer referral agreements, shared operational templates, and collective purchasing power.

### C. Где деньги? (Financial Realism — Correcting the Zoom SaaS Illusion)
* **Zero Premature Software Build:** I4I does **not** spend capital building a complex marketplace or charging premature subscription fees.
* **The Deal-Flow Engine:** I4I operates with near-zero overhead as a curated peer circle and event hub. Its immediate economic value is serving as an **organic, Zero-CAC referral engine** for A4A's operational sprints.
* **Future Monetization:** High-ticket curated mastermind retreats, verified B2B vendor partnerships, and sponsored corporate webinars once critical mass is achieved.
