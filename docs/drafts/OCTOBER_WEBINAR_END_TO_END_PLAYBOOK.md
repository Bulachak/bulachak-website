## 3. Promotion Channels & Distribution Mix

### Channel 1: Direct Personal Outreach (High-Touch / Warm)
* **Who:** Personal sports contacts, fencing club directors, local Tatar community leaders, immigrant business acquaintances.
* **Volume:** 40–50 personalized direct messages.
* **Platform:** WhatsApp / Telegram / Direct SMS.

### Channel 2: Community Channel Broadcasts (Semi-Warm)
* **Who:** US Russian-speaking business groups, immigrant tech and entrepreneur chats, Tatar American Association community chats.
* **Volume:** 3–5 targeted community posts (authorized by admins).

### Channel 3: Targeted Social Outreach (Cold / Semi-Cold)
* **Who:** US-based owners of private sports salles, academies, and boutique studios found via Instagram/LinkedIn.
* **Volume:** 15–20 outbound messages per week starting 14 days before the event.

## 6. The Conversion Offer: The 15-Minute Operational Leak Audit

The webinar does **not** sell the $3,000 sprint from the stage (which creates resistance). It sells the **next micro-step**:

* **Offer:** Free 15-Minute Operational Leak Audit (limited to 8 clubs/businesses).
* **Script at Minute 55:**  
  *«If you want to know where your specific business or academy is leaking 10–15 hours a week, book a 15-minute diagnostic. We'll review your parent communication and intake flow, show you the 3 exact bottlenecks, and hand you a roadmap. If you want us to build the system for you, we can discuss our 21-day sprint. If not, the roadmap is yours to keep.»*
* **Target Conversion Math:**  
  * 35 Registrants → 22 Attendees → 7 Audit Bookings → **2 Closed Turnkey Sprints ($6,000 gross revenue)**.
