The source notes remain research records with their original limits.

# Editing approach

The editing approach drew on these inferences from the readings. They are not universal grammar rules:

| Issue to review | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Abstract noun chains | Name the concrete action and use a complete Tatar sentence. |
| Community vocabulary | Distinguish an organization, a cultural association, society, and personal kinship according to the intended meaning. |
| Technical terminology | Use the selected convention, ясалма интеллект (ЯИ), consistently. The media sample contains other terms but does not establish a preferred abbreviation. |
| Friendly register | Use concise, courteous phrasing. Do not reproduce ceremonial praise, bureaucratic openings, or conversational fillers merely because they occur in published articles. |
| Project voice | Keep Bulachak and its branches as the subjects. Personal quotations in the research do not authorize first-person founder narration on project pages. |
| Philosophy and promises | Preserve co-opetition, human alignment, accurate development status, prospective funding, and the approved name Зөбәрҗәт болын. |
