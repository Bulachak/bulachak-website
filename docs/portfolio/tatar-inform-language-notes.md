# Tatar-Inform language reference notes

Four complete article bodies were read on tatar-inform.tatar. A1, A2, and A3 were opened through the web reader. A4 was read in the browser after the web reader could not retrieve it. Search results alone are not the evidence for these notes. No website translations were changed.

This is a small reference sample, not validation of the website's Tatar. It contains editorial prose and published quotations of human speakers. Publication does not establish whether every quotation was originally spoken in Tatar, translated, or edited. The fencing report especially should be treated as published Tatar sports journalism, not a verified transcript of spontaneous Tatar speech.

## Articles read

### A1. AI as a working tool

[Рәмис Латыйпов: Ясалма фәһемнең уңай якларын өйрәнеп, үз файдабызга дөрес итеп кулланыйк](https://tatar-inform.tatar/news/ramis-latyipov-yasalma-fahemnen-unai-yaklaryn-oiranep-uz-faidabyzga-dores-itep-kullanyik-5899736)

Published **29 January 2025, 18:44**, Society. By Zilya Mubarakshina. The report quotes editor Ramis Latypov describing editorial AI use and the need for human judgment. The article body, including the final remarks about tools and costs, was read.

Short speech sample: “Дөрес юлга кертеп җибәргәндә, ул ярдәмче була ала.”

The conditional action comes before the main predicate. The sentence describes what the tool can do under a stated condition instead of making an abstract claim about transformation. The surrounding quotations use concrete actions such as writing, finding, editing, and making mistakes. The opening report is more formal, with institutional names and attribution. Both AI terms in the vocabulary table occur in this one article; their coexistence is evidence of variation, not permission to alternate inconsistently in product copy.

### A2. Community and exchange of experience

[Милли массакүләм мәгълүмат чарасы форумы вәкиле: Тираж кимү мәсьәләсе һаман урынында тора](https://tatar-inform.tatar/news/milli-massakulam-maglumat-carasy-forumy-vakile-tiraz-kimu-masalase-haman-urynynda-tora-5892526)

Published **29 August 2024, 12:03**, Society. By Zilya Mubarakshina. A report from a media forum combines institutional context, participants' concerns, and remarks about meeting others and exchanging experience. All prose through the program notes was read.

Short speech sample from participant Sabira Abitova: “Үзара аралашабыз, тәҗрибә уртаклашабыз, бик ошый.”

The quotation has a direct rhythm: related finite verbs followed by a short evaluation. It is a better reference for approachable rhythm than the report's long institutional opening. Its first-person voice belongs to the speaker and should not be copied into Bulachak's project pages. The article distinguishes organized cultural associations from broader society. A community label therefore needs the intended relationship specified; one English umbrella word should not automatically produce the same Tatar noun everywhere.

### A3. Clear information about a learning project

[«Ясалма фәһем» онлайн курсында нейрочелтәрләр белән эшләү күнекмәләрен өйрәтәләр](https://tatar-inform.tatar/news/yasalma-fahem-onlain-kursynda-neiroceltarlar-belan-eslau-kunekmalaren-oiratalar-5882720)

Published **5 February 2024, 12:40**, Science and Education. By Guliya Khasbiullina. The article explains a course and quotes organizer Raynur Khasanov about its sequence. The full article, including registration, timing, and recorded lessons, was read.

Short editorial sample: “Теркәлү хәзер дә мөмкин.”

This is service information, not conversational speech: a brief statement makes availability clear. The article combines a project description with concrete next steps and explains specialist content through lessons and tasks. Its final paragraph describes who can view a recording and when. That is a useful website model: say what a visitor can do, then give the condition or timing. The sample does not justify adding a registration action to a website that has none.

### A4. Fencing and joint work

[Фехтование буенча Россия җыелма командасы тренеры Миңнехановка рәхмәт белдерде](https://tatar-inform.tatar/news/fextovanie-buenca-rossiya-yelma-komandasy-trenery-minnexanovka-raxmat-belderde-5910033)

Published **29 August 2025, 12:35**, Sport. No byline was visible. The complete rendered body describes a fencing center opening, quotes coach Ilgar Mamedov, and ends with the organizations involved in its construction.

Short published speech sample: “Зур рәхмәт сезгә.”

The report uses ordinary borrowed sport names with Tatar grammatical endings, names the facility concretely, and states who worked jointly. Its institutional sports vocabulary is useful; the ceremonial praise is not a model for Bulachak's descriptions. The final sentence names the responsible organizations before the completed-action predicate. It reports an actual opening, so its completion language must not be copied into descriptions of P4P's development plans.

## Implications and limits

- Prefer concrete actions and complete Tatar sentence structures over translating an English chain of abstract nouns. This is an editorial inference from the sample, not a general linguistic rule proved by four articles.
- Separate a broad social context, an organized association, and personal kinship before selecting a community term. The sample does not validate the existing immigrant-kinship wording.
- Distinguish news narration, practical service information, and published speech. Official reporting can be grammatical and still too formal for a website. Quoted speech can be lively and still unsuitable as an organization-wide first-person voice.
- Keep Bulachak's agreed project facts and philosophy as the authority for meaning. These articles do not establish a translation of Emerald Pasture, a Tatar equivalent for co-opetition, or specialist parafencing terminology.
- Do not use an AI-generated interview as evidence of native human Tatar. Search surfaced Tatar-Inform's interview with ChatGPT; it was excluded from this reference sample.
- The hidden Tatar version still needs a later sentence-by-sentence review by a proficient Tatar editor, covering meaning, idiom, terminology, and consistency. This research is preparation for that work, not a reason to republish the translation.
