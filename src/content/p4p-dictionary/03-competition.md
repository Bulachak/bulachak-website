## Competition
short: One tournament, such as a World Cup or a Zonal Championships. It contains events, and events contain bouts.
avoid: event (for the whole tournament)
source: World Para Fencing Classification Rules (Book 4), Glossary (Competition); Organisation Rules, January 2026, o.3, o.7, o.8 and o.108.2
status: official

A competition is a series of [[Event|Events]] held together under one ruling body. The official World Para Fencing competitions are the Paralympic Games, the World Championships, the [[World Cup|World Cups]], the [[Zonal Championships]], Satellite Tournaments and Subregional Championships or Games. The type of competition sets the [[Coefficient]] applied to ranking points. The Organisation Rules use other words for the same two levels: they call the whole gathering a tournament and each contest inside it a competition.

## Event
short: One contest inside a competition: one weapon, men, women or mixed, individual or team, and for individual events one category.
avoid: division; competition (for a single weapon and category)
source: World Para Fencing Classification Rules (Book 4), Glossary (Event); Organisation Rules, January 2026, o.7, o.36, o.41 and o.66
status: official

Women's Sabre [[Category B]] at a [[World Cup]] is one event. Most individual events run as [[Pool|Pools]] followed by [[Direct Elimination]]. At the Paralympic Games and the World Championships they run by direct elimination with [[Repechage]] throughout: see [[Individual Event Formula]]. A [[Team Event]] is also an event, with its own entries and results.

## Pool
short: The opening round. A small group where every fencer fences every other fencer, five touches each.
source: World Para Fencing Organisation Rules, January 2026, o.66 to o.69 and o.73; Technical Rules, January 2026, t.38
status: official

A pool [[Bout]] ends when one fencer has scored five [[Touch|Touches]] or when three minutes of fencing time have passed. Pools have seven fencers, or seven and six, and there is a single pool when fewer than ten fencers take part: see [[Pool Composition]]. The results of all pools are turned into one ranking by the [[Pool Indicators]], and that ranking seeds the [[Direct Elimination Table]]. Pools must be fenced up to the last bout.

## Direct Elimination
short: The knockout rounds. Without repechage, one loss ends the event. Bouts go to fifteen touches.
avoid: DE without explanation in athlete-facing text
source: World Para Fencing Organisation Rules, January 2026, o.66, o.76, o.80, o.82, o.90 and o.108.2; Technical Rules, January 2026, t.39 and t.40
status: official

Fencers are placed in a [[Direct Elimination Table]] from the ranking after the [[Pool|Pools]]. Each [[Bout]] is fenced to fifteen [[Touch|Touches]], or until three periods of three minutes have passed, with one minute of rest between periods. A fencer always has ten minutes of rest between two consecutive bouts. The final placing in the [[Event]] is the result that earns [[World Ranking]] points. At the Paralympic Games and the World Championships a first loss leads into the [[Repechage]] table.

## Bout
short: One contest between two fencers in which the score is kept to decide a result.
avoid: match (for two individuals), fight
source: World Para Fencing Organisation Rules, January 2026, o.5 and o.6
status: official

A bout belongs to an [[Event]] and to a stage: [[Pool]], [[Direct Elimination]], or one leg of a [[Team Event]]. It has two fencers, a score in [[Touch|Touches]] and a winner. The rules keep the word match for teams: a [[Team Match]] is the whole series of bouts fenced between two teams.

> Was that a bout or a match?
> Two fencers is a bout. Two teams is a match.

## Team Event
short: A contest between national teams. Called a team event or team competition, never a relay.
avoid: relay, relay event, relay team
source: P4P house style; World Para Fencing Organisation Rules, January 2026, o.6, o.97 to o.99 and o.104
status: rule

Teams meet in a sequence of short bouts scored with the [[Relay Formula]]. The lineup is a [[Team Roster]]. Team events outside the Paralympic program are [[Developmental Team Event|Developmental Team Events]].

## Relay Formula
short: The scoring mechanism inside a team event: the score carries over from bout to bout, five touches at a time.
avoid: using "relay" for the event, the competition or the roster
source: P4P house style; World Para Fencing Organisation Rules, January 2026, o.5, o.99.1, o.99.5 and o.104.1; Technical Rules, January 2026, t.41
status: rule

Each [[Bout]] in a [[Team Event]] raises the target by five [[Touch|Touches]], and the next pair picks up the running score. The formula names the scoring only. The event stays a team event.

## Team Roster
short: The athletes named to a team for a team event, starters and designated understudies.
avoid: relay roster, lineup card, team captain
source: P4P house style; World Para Fencing Organisation Rules, January 2026, o.97 and o.104.2
status: rule

A team roster needs the right mix of [[Category A]] and [[Category B]] fencers for its [[Team Event]].

## World Cup
short: An international competition on the World Para Fencing calendar. Results earn world ranking points.
source: World Para Fencing Organisation Rules, January 2026, o.3, o.41, o.86 and o.108
status: official

A senior World Cup is a [[Competition]] with at least fifteen [[Event|Events]]: twelve individual events ([[Category A]] and [[Category B]] for men and women in each [[Weapon]]) and three [[Team Event|Team Events]], fenced on the last day. [[Category C]] individual events are optional. There is no limit on individual entries, and each country may enter one team per weapon. A fencer's best six World Cup results count toward the [[World Ranking]], with a [[Coefficient]] of 1.0.

## Zonal Championships
short: The championships of one zone. Only the latest one counts toward the world ranking, with a higher coefficient than a World Cup.
source: World Para Fencing Organisation Rules, January 2026, o.3, o.9, o.16.1, o.42, o.60 and o.108
status: official

The senior program has 25 [[Event|Events]]: sixteen individual and nine [[Team Event|Team Events]]. Each federation may enter up to six fencers at each weapon in the individual events, and one team. Results carry a [[Coefficient]] of 1.2, against 1.0 for a [[World Cup]]. The Technical Delegate is chosen from outside the zone, to keep decisions neutral.

## Paralympic Qualification Event
short: A team event at a World Cup or Zonal Championships that counts toward LA28. It is a qualifier, not the Games.
avoid: Paralympic Event, Paralympic Team Event (for anything outside the Games)
source: P4P house style
status: rule

World Cups and [[Zonal Championships]] are not the Paralympic Games. In a competition schedule, name it as a Paralympic Qualification Event followed by the event, for example Mixed Foil Team.

## Developmental Team Event
short: A team event outside the Paralympic program. Official, sanctioned, and valuable for international match experience.
avoid: secondary event
source: P4P house style
status: rule

Developmental team events build squad breadth and give more athletes a [[Team Roster]] spot. They are a [[Team Event]] in every respect and are never described as secondary.
