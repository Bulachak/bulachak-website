## World Ranking
short: The official published list for one weapon, gender and category, or for one team event.
source: World Para Fencing Organisation Rules, January 2026, o.108.1 to o.108.3 and o.109.1; Operational Regulations, version 20251217, section 15.5
status: official

A world ranking adds up the points a fencer or team has earned at each [[Competition]], weighted by the [[Coefficient]] of that competition. Only the [[Counted Results]] go into the total. Points are awarded only in the category the fencer competed in, so a [[Category A]] fencer and a [[Category B]] fencer are never ranked against each other. There are separate senior, U17 and U23 rankings. Each list is kept permanently up to date and is updated automatically once World Para Fencing has verified the results of a counted competition. Unless special rules apply, the current ranking decides all rankings, seedings and exemptions. Teams have their own [[World Team Ranking]].

## Coefficient
short: The weight applied to ranking points for a competition level, such as Coefficient 1.0, 1.2, 2.0 or 3.0.
avoid: multiplier; points multiplier; multiplying factor
source: P4P house style; factors from World Para Fencing Organisation Rules, January 2026, o.108.2
status: rule

The same placing earns more [[World Ranking]] points at a [[Competition]] with a higher coefficient. The rules list the factors 0.1, 0.3, 1.0, 1.2, 2.0 and 3.0: a [[World Cup]] counts at 1.0 and [[Zonal Championships]] at 1.2. See the [[Ranking Points Scale]].

> What is Zonals worth?
> Look at the coefficient for that level. It is higher than a World Cup.

## Quota Place
short: A place at the Games. Under the LA28 rules it is allocated to an individual athlete, not to the nation. In plain speech: a spot.
avoid: berth; berths
source: P4P house style; allocation rule from IPC Los Angeles 2028 Paralympic Games Qualification Regulations, August 2026, Wheelchair Fencing, p. 116
status: rule

Use spots, roster spots, selection spots, or quota places under IPC and World Para Fencing regulations. The IPC regulations call it a [[Qualification Slot]] and state that slots are allocated to individual athletes, not to the [[NPC]]. A quota place is still separate from which [[Event]] the athlete enters, which depends on the [[Maximum Entries per NPC]]. Places are earned through results that count toward the [[Paralympic Ranking List]] and the [[World Ranking]].

> How many berths does the host get?
> Spots. Or quota places, if you are quoting the regulations.

## World Team Ranking
short: The official ranking of national teams in one team event, built from team results.
source: World Para Fencing Organisation Rules, January 2026, o.98.2 to o.98.3 and o.109.1 to o.109.2
status: official

The senior team ranking counts a team's best six Team [[World Cup]] results, the latest World Championships or Paralympic Games, the latest [[Zonal Championships]] and the best Subregional result. Points come from the team line of the [[Ranking Points Scale]], weighted by the same [[Coefficient]] as individual results. The list is updated on a permanent basis, and teams level on points are separated in the same way as fencers in the [[World Ranking]]. In a [[Team Event]], the first four teams are placed in the direct elimination table by this ranking, the other ranked teams are drawn in pairs, and unranked teams take the last places.

## Paralympic Ranking List
short: The World Abilitysport Para Fencing ranking used for LA28 qualification, closing on 31 May 2028.
source: IPC Los Angeles 2028 Paralympic Games Qualification Regulations, August 2026, Wheelchair Fencing, pp. 117 to 124
status: official

The regulations name three kinds of list: the Paralympic Individual Ranking List, the Paralympic Team Ranking List for men's and women's team sabre, and the Paralympic Mixed Team Ranking List for mixed team foil and épée. All of them close on 31 May 2028, and every [[Qualification Slot]] is allocated from their standings. Results count only if they were achieved within the [[Qualification Period]]. The lists also decide entry: an athlete must be in the top 30 to enter a further individual event, and a team must be in the top 14. The regulations sometimes add the word "World" to the list name; it is the same list. It is separate from the ordinary [[World Ranking]].

## Qualification Period
short: The window in which results count toward LA28 qualification: 1 October 2026 to 31 May 2028.
source: IPC Los Angeles 2028 Paralympic Games Qualification Regulations, August 2026, Wheelchair Fencing, pp. 117 and 124
status: official

Athletes and teams can attain performances for the [[Paralympic Ranking List]] from 1 October 2026. The period ends on 31 May 2028 for both individual and team lists. The same window is used for [[Paralympic Eligibility]]: each athlete must have competed in at least three international sanctioned para fencing competitions between those two dates. Results outside the window do not count toward any [[Qualification Slot]], although they may still count toward the ordinary [[World Ranking]].

> When does LA28 qualifying start?
> On 1 October 2026. It closes on 31 May 2028.

## Qualification Slot
short: A place in the LA28 wheelchair fencing quota, allocated to an individual athlete, not to the NPC.
source: IPC Los Angeles 2028 Paralympic Games Qualification Regulations, August 2026, Wheelchair Fencing, pp. 116 and 119 to 124
status: official

This is the regulations' word for a [[Quota Place]]. The total athlete quota is 96: 48 male and 48 female. Slots are allocated in a fixed order: [[Mixed Team Allocation]], [[Sabre Team Allocation]], [[Individual Ranking Allocation]], [[Host Country Allocation]] and finally the [[Bipartite Commission Invitation]]. Each method allocates slots to named athletes from the rankings, never to the [[NPC]] as a block. No NPC may go beyond the [[Maximum Quota per NPC]].

## Paralympic Medal Events
short: The 16 wheelchair fencing medal events at LA28: seven for men, seven for women and two mixed team events.
source: IPC Los Angeles 2028 Paralympic Games Qualification Regulations, August 2026, Wheelchair Fencing, pp. 116 and 118
status: official

Men and women each fence individual [[Foil]], [[Épée]] and [[Sabre]] in [[Category A]] and [[Category B]], plus a sabre team event. The two mixed events are team foil and team épée. Each individual medal event has a maximum of 21 entries, and each [[Team Event]] a maximum of 10 teams. There are no LA28 medal events for Category C. All 16 events share one athlete quota of 96, set out under [[Qualification Slot]].

## Maximum Quota per NPC
short: The most qualification slots one NPC may receive: four male and four female, two of each in Category A and Category B.
source: IPC Los Angeles 2028 Paralympic Games Qualification Regulations, August 2026, Wheelchair Fencing, pp. 116 to 117, 121 and 123
status: official

An [[NPC]] may be allocated no more than four male slots, two in [[Category A]] and two in [[Category B]], and four female slots on the same split. When a team allocation would push an NPC past this limit, it receives slots only up to the limit, and the unused slots go to the Team Sabre Athlete Allocation within [[Sabre Team Allocation]]. In the [[Individual Ranking Allocation]], a slot that an NPC cannot take passes to the next highest ranked athlete in that category and weapon.

## Maximum Entries per NPC
short: How many athletes and teams one NPC may enter in each LA28 event: two per individual event and one team per team event.
source: IPC Los Angeles 2028 Paralympic Games Qualification Regulations, August 2026, Wheelchair Fencing, pp. 117 to 118
status: official

An athlete who qualified from the individual ranking must enter the event in which the slot was won. Athletes who qualified through a team method may enter the individual events in the same weapon as their team event. Any qualified athlete may also enter another individual event if ranked in the top 30 on the [[Paralympic Ranking List]]. If an event is oversubscribed, athletes who won their slot in that event come first. A team needs a top 14 ranking; an NPC without a team slot may still enter a [[Team Event]] if enough of its athletes already hold individual slots. This is separate from the ordinary [[Entry Limit]].

## Mixed Team Allocation
short: The first LA28 allocation method: top mixed foil and mixed épée teams, worldwide and by zone, each qualify four athletes.
source: IPC Los Angeles 2028 Paralympic Games Qualification Regulations, August 2026, Wheelchair Fencing, pp. 119 to 120
status: official

The top team on each Paralympic Mixed Team Ranking List qualifies, then the top team not already qualified in each zone: America/Africa, Europe and Asia/Oceania. Each qualified team brings one male and one female athlete in [[Category A]] and the same in [[Category B]]. Slots go to the [[NPC]]'s highest ranked athletes in that weapon. An NPC that leads both mixed events receives the slots in only one of them, chosen by tie rules that differ slightly between the world and zonal routes, and the other slot passes down. Together the two routes give 16 male and 16 female slots. See [[Paralympic Ranking List]].

## Sabre Team Allocation
short: The LA28 allocation method for men's and women's team sabre: top teams, worldwide and by zone, each qualify three athletes.
source: IPC Los Angeles 2028 Paralympic Games Qualification Regulations, August 2026, Wheelchair Fencing, pp. 121 to 122
status: official

The top team on each Paralympic Team Ranking List for sabre qualifies, then the top team from each zone. The NPC receives slots for its top ranked individual [[Sabre]] athletes, at most two in [[Category A]] and one in [[Category B]], always within the [[Maximum Quota per NPC]]. A Team Sabre Athlete Allocation then uses unused slots so that each sabre [[Team Event]] has at least six teams. Any slots still left may go to the [[Bipartite Commission Invitation]] or to the Category A sabre individual list.

## Individual Ranking Allocation
short: The LA28 allocation method that gives slots to the highest ranked individual athletes not otherwise qualified.
source: IPC Los Angeles 2028 Paralympic Games Qualification Regulations, August 2026, Wheelchair Fencing, pp. 122 to 123
status: official

The regulations call it the Individual Paralympic Ranking List Allocation. It allocates 15 male and 15 female slots from the individual [[Paralympic Ranking List]]. Only athletes whose [[NPC]] has not qualified a team in that weapon can receive a slot. Each NPC may receive one slot per category and gender through this method. The split per weapon is fixed: in [[Category A]], two each in foil and épée and one in sabre; in [[Category B]], three each in foil and épée and four in sabre. When an NPC is at its limit, the slot passes to the next athlete.

> If the top two are from the same country, who gets the second spot?
> The next highest ranked athlete from another NPC.

## Host Country Allocation
short: The six LA28 slots reserved for the host country: three male and three female athletes.
source: IPC Los Angeles 2028 Paralympic Games Qualification Regulations, August 2026, general section p. 4 and Wheelchair Fencing, pp. 123 to 124
status: official

The host country directly qualifies three male athletes, two in [[Category A]] and one in [[Category B]], and three female athletes on the same split, so that it can take part in every [[Team Event]]. These must be its highest ranked athletes on the individual [[Paralympic Ranking List]], and they still have to meet [[Paralympic Eligibility]]. If the host earns slots through other methods, not counting the [[Bipartite Commission Invitation]], its reserved slots shrink accordingly and pass to the Team Sabre Athlete Allocation. The host may earn slots up to the [[Maximum Quota per NPC]].

## Bipartite Commission Invitation
short: An invitation slot that the IPC grants directly to an athlete: one male and one female in LA28 wheelchair fencing.
source: IPC Los Angeles 2028 Paralympic Games Qualification Regulations, August 2026, general section pp. 8 to 9 and Wheelchair Fencing, pp. 122 and 124 to 125
status: official

The IPC grants these invitations at its sole discretion after engaging with World Abilitysport. They count inside the athlete quota. Each invitation belongs to the individual athlete, not to the [[NPC]]; if the athlete drops out, the slot is withdrawn. The athlete must still meet [[Paralympic Eligibility]] and hold a suitable [[Sport Class]]. Decisions are final, with no protest or appeal. The NPC applies in writing to World Abilitysport. The sources disagree on the deadline: the allocation table says 10 June 2028, while the timeline says 30 June 2028. Unused slots from [[Reallocation of Unused Slots]] can also go through this method.

## Confirmation of Slots
short: The written step in which each NPC tells World Abilitysport how many allocated slots it will use.
source: IPC Los Angeles 2028 Paralympic Games Qualification Regulations, August 2026, general section p. 9 and Wheelchair Fencing, p. 125
status: official

World Abilitysport confirms the allocation of each [[Qualification Slot]] to the NPCs in writing by 14 June 2028. By 22 June 2028 each qualified [[NPC]] must confirm in writing, and email is accepted, how many slots it will use. An NPC that does not respond by then loses its slots. By 10 July 2028 NPCs must officially declare their use of the slots, and an NPC that declares a slot and then does not use it may face sanctions. Slots not confirmed go to [[Reallocation of Unused Slots]].

> Is an email enough to confirm?
> Yes. It has to reach World Abilitysport by 22 June 2028.

## Reallocation of Unused Slots
short: What happens to a qualification slot an NPC does not use: it can be passed on, and late vacancies go back to the IPC.
source: IPC Los Angeles 2028 Paralympic Games Qualification Regulations, August 2026, general section p. 9 and Wheelchair Fencing, p. 125
status: official

In wheelchair fencing, an allocated slot that the [[NPC]] does not use may be reallocated through the [[Bipartite Commission Invitation]] method. World Abilitysport confirms all reallocations in writing by 1 July 2028. A slot returned or left unused after 10 July 2028 goes back to the IPC, which may redistribute it to any sport and across gender categories, or decide not to. A redistributed slot also goes to an individual athlete and cannot override eligibility or classification rules. See [[Confirmation of Slots]].

## NPC
short: National Paralympic Committee: the body that enters a nation's qualified athletes in the Paralympic Games.
source: IPC Los Angeles 2028 Paralympic Games Qualification Regulations, August 2026, general section p. 7 and Wheelchair Fencing, pp. 116 to 117
status: official

Only NPCs in good standing with the IPC may enter athletes, and the national federation must be in good standing with its international federation. An NPC may set its own national selection standards, as long as they comply with IPC and international federation rules. In wheelchair fencing the NPC receives slots on behalf of named athletes, but the [[Qualification Slot]] itself belongs to the athlete. Limits per NPC appear in [[Maximum Quota per NPC]] and [[Maximum Entries per NPC]].

## Paralympic Eligibility
short: The conditions an athlete must meet to be entered in LA28 wheelchair fencing: a 2028 licence, three competitions and a valid sport class status.
source: IPC Los Angeles 2028 Paralympic Games Qualification Regulations, August 2026, general section pp. 7 to 8 and Wheelchair Fencing, p. 117
status: official

An athlete must hold a valid World Abilitysport Para Fencing athlete [[Licence]] for 2028. They must have competed in at least three international sanctioned para fencing competitions between 1 October 2026 and 31 May 2028, the [[Qualification Period]]. They must be internationally classified in an eligible [[Sport Class]] with a [[Sport Class Status]] of Confirmed, or Review with a fixed review date after 31 December 2028. The IPC adds general conditions: nationality rules, a signed Conditions of Participation Agreement and an NPC in good standing. There is no general minimum age.
